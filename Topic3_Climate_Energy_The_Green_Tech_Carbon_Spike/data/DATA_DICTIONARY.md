# Data dictionary: Topic 3, The Green Tech Carbon Spike

The city is a mid-sized US city in the Central time zone. Its electricity comes through one balancing authority, the Metro City Power Authority (code `MCPA`). A balancing authority is the grid operator that matches supply and demand for an area every hour.

- **Year 0 = 2022.** The 10,000 electric vehicles (EVs) under the mandate were registered between September and December 2022.
- **Year 1 = 2023.** This is the first full year with all 10,000 EVs on the road.

**Time zones.** The city's local time is US Central (`America/Chicago`): UTC-6 in standard time, UTC-5 in daylight time. Daylight saving time began on 2022-03-13 and 2023-03-12 (clocks jumped from 02:00 to 03:00) and ended on 2022-11-06 and 2023-11-05 (clocks went back from 02:00 to 01:00).

| File | Rows | One row is | Time zone of the timestamps |
|---|---|---|---|
| `grid_hourly.csv` | 17,520 | one hour of the balancing authority's demand and generation | UTC, end of the hour |
| `fuel_factors.csv` | 8 | one fuel or source | none |
| `weather_hourly.csv` | 17,520 | one hourly reading at the city's weather station | local clock time, start of the hour |
| `charging_sessions.csv.gz` | about 2.9 million | one charging session of one of the 10,000 EVs | local clock time |
| `vehicles.csv` | 10,000 | one EV registered under the mandate | none (months are local) |
| `stations.csv` | 239 | one public charging station | none (dates are local) |
| `fleet_gasoline_monthly.csv` | 24 | one month of gasoline sales in the city | none (months are local) |

Each large file has a `*_sample.csv` holding its first 1,000 rows.

## grid_hourly.csv

Shaped like the U.S. EIA Hourly Electric Grid Monitor (Form EIA-930) for one balancing authority.

| Column | Type | Units / values | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `utc_time_at_end_of_hour` | text `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` | UTC | The end of the hour the row describes. The row labelled `2022-01-01 07:00` covers 06:00 to 07:00 UTC, which is 00:00 to 01:00 local time on 2022-01-01. The last row is `2024-01-01 06:00`. |
| `balancing_authority` | text | `MCPA` | Balancing authority code. |
| `demand_forecast_mwh` | integer | MWh | The operator's day-ahead forecast of demand for the hour. |
| `demand_mwh` | integer | MWh | Electricity used in the area during the hour: homes, businesses, industry, street lights and EV chargers together. |
| `net_generation` | number | see `unit` | Electricity generated by the authority's own plants during the hour (the sum of the fuel columns). |
| `total_interchange` | number | see `unit` | Net flow to neighbouring balancing authorities. Positive means electricity sent out; negative means electricity received (net imports). Demand equals net generation minus total interchange. |
| `coal`, `natural_gas`, `nuclear`, `wind`, `solar`, `hydro`, `other` | number | see `unit` | Net generation by fuel. `other` is landfill gas and biomass. |
| `unit` | text | blank, `MWh`, `GWh` | Unit of the columns from `net_generation` to `other` in this row. The column was added to the export in 2023, so rows from the earlier export leave it blank. `demand_mwh` and `demand_forecast_mwh` are always in MWh. |

Not recorded: which individual plant ran, what each plant emitted, fuel prices, and which plant would have supplied one more MWh.

## weather_hourly.csv

Hourly readings from one weather station in the city.

| Column | Type | Units / values | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `timestamp` | text `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` | local clock time, start of the hour | The hour of the reading as shown on a clock in the city. On the days daylight saving time ended, 01:00 appears twice. On the days it began, 02:00 does not appear. |
| `station_id` | text | `WX-0417` | Weather station. |
| `temp_f` | number | degrees Fahrenheit | Air temperature. |
| `heating_degree_hours` | number | degree-hours, base 65 F | 65 minus the temperature when the temperature is below 65 F, otherwise 0. |
| `wind_speed_mph` | integer | miles per hour | Wind speed at the station, 10 m above the ground; 0 means calm. |
| `relative_humidity` | integer | percent | Relative humidity. |

Not recorded: cloud cover, sunshine, rain and snow. The authority's wind farms are not at this station.

## charging_sessions.csv.gz

Every charging session of the 10,000 mandate EVs, from the city's charging data platform. Public networks and home chargers both report to this platform. The file is gzip-compressed CSV.

| Column | Type | Units / values | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `session_id` | text `CS########` | | Session identifier. Each session should appear once. |
| `vehicle_id` | text `EV#####` | | The EV that charged; links to `vehicles.csv`. |
| `station_id` | text | `ST#####` or `H-EV#####` | A public station (links to `stations.csv`) or the owner's home charger (`H-` followed by the vehicle id). |
| `port_type` | text | `Level 1`, `Level 2`, `DC Fast` | Charger level: 120 V household outlet, 240 V charger, or DC fast charger. |
| `connector` | text | `J1772`, `CCS`, `CHAdeMO`, `NACS` | Plug used. |
| `start_time` | text `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` | local clock time | When energy started to flow. |
| `end_time` | text `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` | local clock time | When energy stopped flowing. Energy flows at a steady rate between start and end. |
| `kwh` | number | kWh | Energy delivered in the session, measured at the charger. |
| `home_or_public` | text | `home`, `public` | Where the session took place. |

Not recorded: when the car was plugged in and unplugged, miles driven, battery level, price paid, and charging by EVs outside the mandate.

## fleet_gasoline_monthly.csv

Gasoline sold at the city's fuel stations, from the state's monthly fuel-tax reports.

| Column | Type | Units | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `month` | text `YYYY-MM` | | Calendar month. |
| `gallons_sold` | integer | gallons | Gasoline sold in the city that month, all vehicles together. |
| `avg_price_usd` | number | US dollars per gallon | Average pump price. |
| `stations_reporting` | integer | | Number of fuel stations that filed a report. |

Not recorded: miles driven, gasoline bought outside the city, and diesel.

## How the files relate

- `charging_sessions.vehicle_id` links to `vehicles.vehicle_id`. For public sessions, `charging_sessions.station_id` links to `stations.station_id`.
- `grid_hourly` and `weather_hourly` are both hourly; their time zones are listed in the table at the top.
- `fleet_gasoline_monthly` is monthly.
