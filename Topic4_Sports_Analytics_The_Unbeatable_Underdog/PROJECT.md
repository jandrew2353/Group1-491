# Topic 4. Sports Analytics: The "Unbeatable" Underdog

## The problem

A model is all-in on a team nobody saw coming. In a college basketball dataset, historically weak Team C is winning 85% of its games, well ahead of powerhouse Team A at 70%. A basic algorithm has picked Team C to win the national championship easily, and real money follows models like this one. Decide whether Team C is a true contender or whether the win rate is lying.

## Inputs from the domain experts

1. A win counts the same in the standings, whoever the opponent is.
2. Most of a team's games are played against teams in its own conference.
3. Each team arranges its own non-conference games before the season starts.
4. Home teams win more often than visiting teams; a game at a neutral site has no home team.
5. A preseason rating is published for every team before its first game, and it is not updated during the season.
6. Tournament seeds reflect the selection committee's view of how strong each team is.
